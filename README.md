Each method behaves differently under load, water and soil movement, and each suits different areas. ## Why Method Selection Matters

Canberra paving is exposed to:

- Reactive soils that move with moisture
- Winter frost cycles
- Heavy summer storms
- Vehicle loads on driveways
- Tree roots
- Wet conditions around pools

The method decides how a surface handles each of these.

## Method Overview

| Method | Construction | Behaviour | Pavers Reusable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segmental | Road base + bedding sand | Flexible | Yes, can be lifted and relaid |
| Rigid | Concrete base + mortar or glue | Fixed | Removable but not reusable |
| Permeable | Permeable pavers + suitable base | Drains through surface | — |

## Failure Modes by Cause

| Cause | Effect | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect compaction | Sinking | Specified, compacted base |
| Poor drainage | Bedding washout, movement | Falls, channel drains, pit boxes |
| Failed edging | Spreading, open joints | Mortared concrete edge |
| Excess loads | Rutting, cracking | Vehicle-rated depth and base |
| Tree roots | Lifting | Method choice, root management |

## Repair Intervention Levels

| Trigger | Intervention |
|---|---|
| Loose, broken or missing pavers | Replace or re-bed |
| Level change > 15mm | Replace or re-bed |
| Sinking with pooling | Re-level, replace or re-bed |
| Slippery (oil or moss) | Pressure wash, replace or re-bed |
| Water pooling | Channel drain or pit box |
| Tree root damage | Cut roots, replace segment |
